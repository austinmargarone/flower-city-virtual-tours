## Village Gate Square Facts

Located in **NOTA**. Rochester’s **Neighborhood of The Arts.**

**NOTA** welcomes visitors and newcomers

### Other places of interest in NOTA

The MAG Memorial Art Gallery
George Eastman Museum
The International Museum of Photography
Writers and Books
Visual Studies Workshop

Village Gate and its next-door neighbor, **The Fabrics and Finding Building** houses some of **Rochester’s most talented individual artists**.

**Artists open their studios to visitors the 2nd Saturday of each month**.

**Free Parking is available.**

**Mixed use idea** of loft living and commercial retail space available.

Lots of great **Restaurants** are available at the Village Gate.

## In Conclusion

The Village Gate Square and the Village Gate offers a destination for you to explore some amazing restaurants and art in one location. Be sure to give yourself enough time to explore all that this area has to offer. The Artist Open Studio Monthly Event on the 2nd Saturday is a great way to meet some local talent.

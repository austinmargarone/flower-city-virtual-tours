The 39-acre **Ontario Beach Park** is located at 4800 Lake Avenue. It contains one of the Great Lakes best natural sand beaches. The park attracts tens of thousands of visitors annually.

## Charlotte Pier

![Ontario Beach Park, Rochester NY, photo 2](/blog-images/2022/02/image-2-682x1024.jpeg)

The 721 meters long **Charlotte Pier** is located at 1 Beach Avenue. The Pier offers a half-mile of access to both the Genesee River and Lake Ontario. Its easy accessibility and ample parking make the pier a hotspot for shore fishermen. You will find people walking the pier all 4 Seasons of the Year.

![Ontario Beach Park, Rochester NY, photo 3](/blog-images/2022/02/image-3-1024x682.jpeg)

View of the Pier from the beach.

The **Port of Rochester** is located on Lake Ontario where the Genesee River meets the lake, about 9 miles North of downtown Rochester. It offers restaurants, boat rides, boat slips and facilities available for rent.

![Ontario Beach Park, Rochester NY, photo 9](/blog-images/2022/02/image-7-1024x682.jpeg)

The 1905 Historic Gustav Dentzel Menagerie **Carousel** is housed in the original building and is located in Ontario Beach Park. The beautiful carousel with 52 hand-carved animals and two chariots is open 7 days a week, the season runs June 28th-September 6th 12:00 pm-7:00 pm. The cost is $1 per ride.

![Ontario Beach Park, Rochester NY, photo 10](/blog-images/2022/02/image-10-1024x682.jpeg)

Celebrating a 200th Anniversary, America’s oldest surviving lighthouse on Lake Ontario, The **Charlotte Genesee Lighthouse** is a historical landmark located at 70 Lighthouse Street, [tours are available.](https://geneseelighthouse.org/)

![Ontario Beach Park, Rochester NY, photo 11](/blog-images/2022/02/image-9-682x1024.jpeg)

The Performance Pavilion. This year marks the 36th Season of the popular summer concert series, *Concerts by the Shore* at Ontario Beach Park, at 7 pm on Wednesdays. A list of performers should be available on the [website](http://www.ontariobeachentertainment.org/). in late Spring.

![Ontario Beach Park, Rochester NY, photo 14](/blog-images/2022/02/image-12-682x1024.jpeg)

**Roger Robach Community Center** is available for weddings, parties and picnics. It is located beachfront at 180 Beach Ave. The Ontario Beach Park Program Committee holds Big Band Dances every Wednesday during the Spring (April/May) and Fall (September/October). The OBPPC Information Line: (585)865-3320.

![Ontario Beach Park, Rochester NY, photo 15](/blog-images/2022/02/image-13-1024x682.jpeg)

*The Charlotte Beach House sits along the shoreline reminding you of days gone by.*

![Ontario Beach Park, Rochester NY, photo 16](/blog-images/2022/02/image-14-1024x682.jpeg)

A terrific 40-page Historical PDF *“[This is where I love to go](https://www.academia.edu/10322695/_This_is_where_I_love_to_go_The_Re_creation_of_Place_at_Ontario_Beach_Park_and_the_Monroe_County_Lakeshore)”: The (Re)creation of Plat at Ontario Beach Park* (v75i1.pdf) by K. Somerville is available if you want to learn more about the Ontario Beach Park.

![Ontario Beach Park, Rochester NY, photo 17](/blog-images/2022/02/image-15-682x1024.jpeg)

Supervised swimming is offered each day from the Friday after Father’s Day through Labor Day 11 am-7 pm; for water conditions call (585)753-5887. Glass, pets, and amplified music are not allowed.

![Ontario Beach Park, Rochester NY, photo 18](/blog-images/2022/02/image-16-682x1024.jpeg)

Located at the Roger Robach Community Center on Beach Avenue, 6 Pickleball nets open for the season in early May-October.

![Ontario Beach Park, Rochester NY, photo 19](/blog-images/2022/02/image-17-682x1024.jpeg)

Be sure to walk around the building and take in the gorgeous architectural details.

Take Note: If you are aiming for a longer walk, **Turning Point Park and Trail**, a nationally acclaimed public works project, is a short distance away. It is a beautiful scenic walk along the river. The www.cityofrochester.gov states: “The trail consists of 3-main parts: 1) the 2,986 ft. land-based trail that utilized an old railroad bed to transition from the top of the bank to the river’s edge. 2) a 3,572 ft.-long bridge over the Genesee River Turning Basin, and 3) an all-new land-based trail, 3,406 ft in length, through Turning Point Park North and adjacent to the Genesee Marina.”

## Seabreeze Amusement Park Facts

Opened on August 5, 1879

Previous Names: Sea Breeze Grove (1879-1900) Sea Breeze Park (1901-1938) Dreamland Park (1939-1974) Seabreeze (1975-Present)

Operation Season: May-September

35 acres located in Irondequoit, NY

Features a complete amusement park and a water park.

4th Oldest operating amusement park in the US.

13th Oldest operating amusement park in the world.

Home to a variety of roller coasters and rides, food concessions, midway games, an arcade, a museum, live entertainment, and picnic areas.

The water park features a variety of waterslides, a lazy river, spray grounds, a wave pool, sunbathing areas, a bathhouse, a retail location, and food outlets.

25 amusement rides including the famous Jack Rabbit.

The Jack Rabbit is the 4th oldest operating roller coaster in the world.

The Jack Rabbit is the 2nd oldest operating roller coaster in the US.

The Jack Rabbit is an “out-and-back” wooden roller coaster.

The Jack Rabbit celebrated its 100th Anniversary in 2020.

Certified Life Guards are on staff. For more information on the [History of Seabreeze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seabreeze_Amusement_Park).

## In Conclusion

Old-fashioned Summer fun is found at [Seabreeze Amusement Park](https://seabreeze.com/). Ride the Jack Rabbit and see what a wooden roller coaster sounds and feels like

Cobbs Hill Park located in the city’s southeast area features a 100-acre sanctuary filled with large oaks and wildlife. At the highest point, you'll find great views of the city of Rochester and the surrounding area. The Reservoir’s walkways, iron fence, steps, and overlooks were designed by the Olmsted firm.

![Cobbs Hill Park, Rochester NY, photo 2](/blog-images/2022/04/image-6-683x1024-1.jpg)

## PARK HIGHLIGHTS

-Cobbs Hill Reservoir provides a .7-mile paved walking path around the sparkling water-filled basin

-Lake Riley

-[MVP Health Care Fitness Court](https://nationalfitnesscampaign.com/) the 30-piece outdoor circuit training system

-Walking trails (Dogs welcome, Bikes are prohibited on trails) [alltrails.com](https://alltrails.com/parks/us/new-york/cobbs-hill-park)

-Sports fields for baseball, softball, and soccer

-Basketball and Tennis Courts

-Playground

-Washington Grove is a popular birding spot filled with ancient oaks

-Geodetic Marker is located on the ground behind the water tower

-Gatehouse was designed by J. F. Warner

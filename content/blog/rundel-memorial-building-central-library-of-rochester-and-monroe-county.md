## Rundel Memorial Building | Central Library of Rochester and Monroe County Facts

The Central Library of Rochester and Monroe County consists of two buildings.

The Rundel Memorial Library building opened in 1936.

The Bausch & Lomb Public Library building opened in 1997.

The library offers a large selection of books, magazines, music, movies and more.

Quiet study places, public meeting rooms, a variety of free programs and opportunities for community engagement are available at the library.

See the [website](https://roccitylibrary.org/location/central/) to discover the Collections available.

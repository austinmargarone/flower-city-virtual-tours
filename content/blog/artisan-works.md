## About

### Mission

To be a place where people experience creation and inspiration.

### Vision

To be a major supporter of the creative community. To help other cities replicate this unique, nonprofit, self-funding model.

### Location

565 Blossom Road, Suite L Rochester, NY 14610

### Hours

Friday-Sunday: 12:00pm-5:00pm

### Note

Open 7 days for scheduled group tours and private events.

Free introductory public tours are offered Saturday/Sunday at 2:00 pm

### Admission

Adults: $15.00

Seniors: $10.00

Students: $10.00

Children (5 and under): Free

Members: Free

### Contact

(585)288-7170 or/ [Website](https://artisanworks.net)

## ARTISANworks Facts

40,000sqft. renovated factory building

Roughly 500,000 items in the collection

Supports regional artists through the art incubator program

Self-funded not-for-profit space existing without government aid

You can watch on YouTube ARTISANworks “A Work in Progress”

ARTISANworks is fully accessible to visitors using wheelchairs, including entrances, exhibits, and restrooms

Create timeless memories with the perfect event at ARTISANworks see the website for details on Event Space Rentals.

Did you know that [ARTISANworks](https://artisanworks.net/) offers a lease-to-own art program see the website for details on buying or leasing art?

## In Conclusion

ARTISANworks is a unique experience. While in Rochester be sure to check it out. You just might find that a piece of art is just the thing to brighten up that bare wall at home.

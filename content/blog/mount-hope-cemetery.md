## Mount Hope Cemetery Facts

Established in 1838.

One of the most remarkable Victorian cemeteries in America.

A magnificent 196-acre of lofty hills and picturesque valleys.

Created by glaciers and transformed into a beautiful historic cemetery.

A diverse forest of trees forms an arboretum.

The cemetery is the permanent resting place of over 350,000 people.

The annual growth rate is 500-600 burials per year.

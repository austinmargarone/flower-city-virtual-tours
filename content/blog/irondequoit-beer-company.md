A hidden treasure is located in the town of Irondequoit. In a lovely restored barn, you will find IBC. Great beer and food await your arrival. Sit in the cozy indoor dining or the large heated outdoor space.

## Irondequoit Beer Company Facts

IBC is located in a beautifully restored barn in the town of Irondequoit.

Ten perfectly crafted brews are available on tap.

Indoor and heated outdoor seating are available for your comfort.

A full menu of delicious food is waiting for you to enjoy. See the menus online.

Brewer, Bobby Baker

Private event space is available.

Check out the: merchandise, mug club, and gift cards online.

This gem is a local favorite. It’s a fun spot to hang out with friends and enjoy a bite and a brew.

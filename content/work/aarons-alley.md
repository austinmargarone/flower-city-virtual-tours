We had the opportunity to work with Aaron's Alley, Rochester's local hippie shop! What is more fun than the shop itself are the people who work there. They are so welcoming and ready to assist you as soon as you walk through the door. Elyssa came to us with the idea of a "Walk & Shop" where you can virtually access their retail store. The process was very exciting and has taken their brick and mortar shop to the next level. Check out the Walk & Shop below and don't forget to go visit them in person too!

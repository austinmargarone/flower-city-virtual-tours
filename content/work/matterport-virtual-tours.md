## What is Matterport?

Matterport is the technology that we use to create digital twins. Digital twins are 3D virtual representations of physical spaces. These are also considered virtual tours and are immersive models that can be easily shared with others. Below is our demo that showcases some of the extra features like tags that can add extra functionality to your tour.

## Industries Served

Our digital twins are beneficial for many industries. Some examples include real estate, commercial buildings, showrooms, retail stores and event venues.
